Privacy

What ShiftLens stores about your bot, what it sends to other services, and how to delete it.

What is stored

  • Each answer your bot sends: the question, the answer, two embeddings computed on our own server, and the optional fields you send (request id, prompt and model version, status, latency). A session id is stored only as a hash.
  • What ShiftLens works out from them: flags, incidents, investigation notes, check verdicts, daily digests and weekly reports.
  • Your bot's name, its API key as a hash (the key itself is shown once and never stored), and its Slack webhook if you add one (never shown again or logged).
  • Never stored: the context (documents) you pass to track(context=...), the text of questions you ask about an incident, and anything you paste into "Try without code".

Where

The API runs on Render and the database on Supabase, both in Singapore. This site is static and served by Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages; it uses no cookies and no analytics. Your API key and theme are kept in your browser's local storage.

What is sent to other services

  • Groq (open-weight models): example questions and answers for an incident's investigation note; for a sampled share of answers, the question, the answer and its context for a spot-check; questions you ask about an incident; and the answers you paste into "Try without code".
  • Munito AI, only when these extra checks are turned on: full-coverage checks send the question, the answer and its context; topic names send a few example questions; weekly reports send the week's numbers and the bot's name. Munito AI says it keeps no prompts or outputs and does not train on them.
  • Slack: incident messages, digests and weekly reports, only to webhooks you or the site owner set.

Deleting

DELETE /company with your API key deletes your bot and everything stored for it (see the API docs). A bot that sends no answers is deleted automatically 7 days after it registers.
